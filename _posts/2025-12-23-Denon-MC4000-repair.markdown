## Introduction

The [Denon MC4000]{https://www.denondj.com/mc4000-mc4000xus.html} is a 2-channel DJ Controller, with a good selection of features, and very conveniently works with VirtualDJ using a "cheap" licence, rather than needing an expensive professional licence, which some other DJ Controllers require.

I was asked to repair one, which had the following problems:
- The crossfader no longer worked reliably; either VirtualDJ would not respond to the crossfader movements, or the crossfader would act erratically
- The transport controls (CUE, Play/Pause) as well as SHIFT and SYNC buttons required a heavy press to respond, despite having a tactile click

Since there isn't any information on repairing this item on the internet, I'm going to try and write this up as an instructional how-to guide.

## Step 1

Remove all knobs - all twisty knobs and faders, top and front. Pull them, and they'll come off.

- Top Twisty Knobs: x25
- Top Fader Knobs: x5
- Front Twisty Knobs: x7
